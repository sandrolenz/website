01. About Me

Welcome! I am Sandro Lenz, a Network Specialist and Developer based in Switzerland.
Having completed my computer science apprenticeship in 2023, I have built a strong foundation in IT, ranging from network infrastructure to full-stack development. Highlights of my apprenticeship include serving as Project Lead for a corporate translator tool (using Angular and DeepL) and gaining international experience working at our Bangalore office in the summer of 2022 (You can read our blog about this on apprentices-abroad.com).

When I am not at work, you can find me coding stuff like this website, playing video games or performing with my local music society on the Es-Horn.
